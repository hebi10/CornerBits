# Coastal life · A concept

The latest approved world is a colored pixel coastal place. v0.9.1 keeps the approved A scenery and continuous assigned work, with current job time and earned amount always visible beside the job. Rewards settle every 10 or 15 minutes. The previous monochrome three-game implementation remains in Git and the legacy entry for data safety.

## Visual world

- One coherent miniature: timber home, rust roof, large leafy tree, dock, boat, turquoise water and broken shoreline. Transparent outside the actual island silhouette, no rectangular water or sky background.
- Pixel art follows the approved A board. Quiet natural palette: sea teal/sage, sand/linen, ochre/rust and charcoal timber. UI ink#304940, readable temporary surface#f5efe3. Do not grayscale the new raster assets.
- All art comes from the built-in image generator: `coast-island.png`, `coast-actor.png` (4×4), `coast-props.png` (4×4 first 8), plus `coast-island-level2.png` and `coast-island-level3.png`. `coast-prompts.json` and `coast-growth-prompts.json` preserve exact prompts, sources, alpha and measured anchors. Align at display time; do not repaint or trim generated pixels manually.
- Growth keeps the island, door, paths, dock, tree and boat anchored. Level 2 repairs and extends the timber home; level 3 adds a glass workroom. Keep flowerpot, lantern and bench in the prop layer so their actual saved ownership determines visibility, rather than baking them into growth backgrounds.
- True1× canvas is280×280 inside the280×380 widget. No scaling the whole UI to fake its actual size. The actor is a small adult figure, with feet fixed to the projected paths/dock/beach. Props sit in the same perspective and scale as the house.

## Interaction and motion

- Dock, beach and home are real invisible-background buttons with native tooltip names and visible keyboard focus. Pointer interaction stays inside the scene; no three-game nav or full resource HUD.
- Assign the dock or beach with one click. The person walks through the path, works and returns within each chosen 10-minute or 15-minute cycle, then continues the same job. Confirm each cycle's reward once after saving succeeds. Home level multiplies the base yield of 2 per 10 minutes or 3 per 15 minutes. The rack reflects held fish and becomes empty after sale; selling for 15 shell coins per fish remains available during work. Gathered wood and shell coins enable home projects. Completed decorations and home growth remain visibly placed after restarting or restoring.
- A few local water glints and rising pixel smoke provide quiet ambient motion. Do not float or rotate the whole island. Hidden documents, compact state and home/record menus rest scene motion while work time continues, including behind other applications. Explicit pause, a blocked save and restore preview freeze work time. Reduced motion disables ambient motion and simplifies walking. Resume saved elapsed time and exclude closed-time rewards.
- Tiny top controls provide drag, pause, records, compact and native close. Plain text controls have no fill or border; essential text over wallpaper uses a thin light keyline. Keep the save indicator quiet. No hover decoration, box shadows or rounded cards. Font weight 500/at most 700, maximum 34px; current controls 9px and scene status 10px at logical 1×.
- A persistent work line shows the current job's total time and earned amount, such as **채집 12:34 · 나무 +2**, with a plain **작업 중단** control. Accumulate time across reward cycles and increase the amount only on confirmed settlement. Selling leaves the earned amount intact. Stopping retains materials and clears unfinished cycle time and job counters; the next job starts at zero. Delayed ticks settle completed cycles together. Keep recent settlement details in records and omit cycle alerts from the scene. Do not send Windows notifications or sound.
- Home and record menus are readable linen overlays opened only on request. The home menu shows fish/wood/coins, three prop-led projects with costs/lock/ownership states, home expansion and fish sale. During assigned work it offers **작업 중단**; crafting and expansion require work to stop. Resource counts and construction choices belong here, with only the small job line on the resting scene.
- Records contain save status, a concise progress summary, 10-minute/15-minute **정산 간격** controls, recent settlement details, backup export/import, restore preview/confirmation and save retry. Interval changes require no active job or project. Explain that schema9 preserves existing progress and starts the new counters from saved cycle time and earned amount zero, schema8 also receives the 10-minute interval, and versions 1–7 start the coast fresh. Errors stay visible and actionable; failed saving freezes progress without awarding unpersisted rewards. Never silently replace an unreadable save with a fresh game.
- schema10 saves job total time `workTimeMs` and earned amount `workAmount` alongside the interval, latest settlement and existing coast. Preserve exact counters through restart and backup. Keep elapsed display time in memory and checkpoint every 60 seconds, at work start/reward/stop/interval change/sale/project changes and before hide/compact/backup/exit. Retry the exact failed snapshot. Preserve the original schema8/9 save before migration and the current record before restore. Do not infer past job totals. If the next reward would exceed storage limits, stop the job and clear cycle time and counters while retaining resources, home and decorations.
